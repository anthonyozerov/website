This page helps you find a non-reserved tennis court in Berkeley, CA for walk-up play today. The data should be updated every 10 minutes—thanks to my friend João Vitor for helping make the scraper fast enough for this! Times are in Berkeley's local time.

The data come from the city's reservation website. We check each court's reservation hours for today's date, including seasonal changes:

  • Available (green): a court to try for free walk-up play. The hour is unreserved, outside the court's seasonal booking hours, or on a designated walk-up court.
  • Reserved / blocked: within reservation hours, but not listed as available. It may be reserved, used for a program, or closed.
  • Available + times (yellow): only the listed portion of the hour is free, such as 15:00–15:15.
  • Unknown: we could not verify the reservation data.

These are reservation records, not a live occupancy check: other walk-up players may already be on court. For unlit courts, the natural-light column shows daylight separately from the city's booking hours. Sunrise and sunset are estimates, not court closing times.
